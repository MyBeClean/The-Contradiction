# The Contradiction

> **A world that seems random, yet follows rules hidden beneath the chaos.**

**The Contradiction** is a psychological horror game built around a simple idea:
what if reality itself began to contradict its own rules?

The game begins as an ordinary daily routine. Everything feels familiar, predictable, and normal. But as the player continues exploring, small inconsistencies begin to appear.

Nothing seems completely random.

There is a pattern.

And the deeper the player goes, the more difficult it becomes to understand what is real.

---

## 🎮 Game Concept

The Contradiction focuses on:

* Psychological horror
* Environmental storytelling
* Exploration and puzzles
* Reality-based anomalies
* Hidden rules and patterns
* Gradual changes in the environment
* A normal world slowly becoming impossible

The goal is not to immediately overwhelm the player with horror.

Instead, the game starts normally and slowly introduces things that **should not be possible**.

---

## 🌎 The World

The world of **The Contradiction** may appear chaotic and unpredictable.

However, every strange event follows a hidden rule.

Objects may behave incorrectly.
Spaces may stop making sense.
Gravity may no longer behave normally.
Familiar locations may gradually become unfamiliar.

What looks like randomness may actually be a system the player has not yet understood.

> **Randomness is only a pattern that has not been discovered.**

---

## 📖 Story

The player begins an ordinary day at home.

After receiving a message from a friend named **Samuel**, the player is asked to help with an unusual research project involving something called **"The Contradiction."**

At first, it seems like an abstract scientific concept.

But after Samuel disappears, the player begins to discover that his research may have caused something far more serious.

Something has started affecting reality itself.

The player must investigate what happened while dealing with a world that slowly stops following the rules it once had.

---

## 🕹️ Gameplay

The gameplay is designed around gradual discovery.

### Normal

The player performs ordinary activities and explores familiar environments.

### Strange

Small inconsistencies begin appearing.

### Impossible

The environment starts behaving in ways that should not be possible.

### Contradiction

The player discovers that these events are connected by rules that are difficult to understand.
